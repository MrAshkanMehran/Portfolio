Yatzee Casino Game

A browser-based dice game inspired by the classic Yahtzee experience. Built with JavaScript, HTML, and CSS, featuring interactive gameplay, dynamic scoring, and a sleek gold-and-black casino theme.

Rules

  • Six rounds total.
  • Roll up to three times per round.
  • Click dice to hold them between rolls.
  • Select a scoring option or roll again.

Points

  • Three of a kind: Sum of all dice
  • Four of a kind: Sum of all dice
  • Full house: 25 points
  • Small straight: 30 points
  • Large straight: 40 points
